Three core problems. One app.

Where contractors lose margin every week.

Each of these happens quietly, on every project, until closeout surfaces the damage.

01 — Receipts

Snap. AI extracts. Confirm.

Point your camera at any receipt or invoice. Vendor, total, and taxes pulled automatically. Assigned to the right project in one tap. No manual entry. No lost paper.

02 — Crew

Know who's on site. Right now.

QR code punch-in/out with GPS verification at every event. See every crew member, their active project, and how long they've been clocked in — live on your phone.

03 — Costs

Know your margin before closeout.

Labour, materials, overhead, and running totals per project — updated the moment a receipt is scanned or a session ends. Export to CSV and send to your accountant.
