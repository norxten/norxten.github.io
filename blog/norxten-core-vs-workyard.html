Norxten Core vs Workyard

By Norxten Technologies Inc. · September 27, 2026 · 7 min read

Workyard and Norxten Core both track GPS crew time for construction businesses. Past that, they start from different problems. Workyard starts from GPS time tracking and workforce management, with job costing and labour cost reporting added at its Pro tier. Norxten Core starts from real-time field cost visibility: receipt expenses, crew labour, budget status, and project health, in one app at a flat monthly price.

Here is a factual side-by-side using each company's own published pricing as of September 2026, including where Workyard has the edge, so you can decide based on what your crew actually needs.

Pricing by crew size

Workyard's pricing has two parts: a per-user rate and a flat $50/month base fee on top, on annual billing. Norxten Core has no per-user charge, just a flat monthly price with an employee cap per tier.

If you are comparing on receipts and job costing

Workyard lets a crew member attach a photo or note to a time card. Norxten Core reads the receipt automatically, pulling the vendor and amount and assigning it to the right project without manual entry. If receipt handling and live cost tracking are the main reason you are shopping, this is the core difference between the two tools, not a minor feature gap.

Who should choose Workyard

If GPS time tracking, scheduling, and payroll export are your main need, and job costing is a secondary feature you would use occasionally, Workyard is a mature, well-built option with real depth in that specific area, including facial-photo verification and offline capture on select tiers.

Who should choose Norxten Core

If receipts, live job costs, and a bill that does not go up every time you hire someone within your plan's crew limit are what you are actually trying to solve, Norxten Core was built around that specifically, with OCR receipt scanning included from the Starter tier up.

Frequently asked questions

Does Workyard scan receipts with OCR?

Workyard lets crew attach photos and notes to a time card. Automated OCR extraction of receipt data, such as vendor and amount, is not listed in Workyard's published feature set as of September 2026.

Does Workyard include job costing?

Job costing and labour cost reporting are part of Workyard's Pro tier and above, not the Starter tier.

How much does Workyard cost for a 10-person crew?

On annual billing, Workyard Starter is $6 per user plus a $50 monthly base fee, so a 10-person crew costs $110/month. Workyard Pro, which adds job costing, is $13 per user plus the $50 base fee, or $180/month for 10 people.

Does Norxten Core charge per user?

No. Norxten Core uses flat monthly plan pricing with an employee cap per tier: Starter up to 5 employees at $49.99/month, Field up to 15 employees at $89.99/month, and Pro with unlimited employees at $189.99/month.
