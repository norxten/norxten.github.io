Six fonctionnalités, une seule app. Tout est connecté du premier pointage jusqu'au rapport final.

01

Suivi des coûts de chantier

See exactly what every job costs as it happens — labour, materials, and subcontractors in one place.

02

Visibilité sur les coûts d'équipe

Know your real labour cost per project, not just hours worked. Track against your budget automatically.

03

Capture de reçus et factures

Snap a photo. OCR reads the vendor, amount, and date. Cost lands in the right project immediately.

04

Feuilles de temps en temps réel

Every punch-in and punch-out recorded instantly. See live hours per employee, per project, updated as work happens.

05

Pointage avec vérification GPS

Crew members punch in and out from the app, with GPS verification at every punch event. See who is clocked in, which project they are on, and when hours are recorded.

06

Rapports de clôture de projet

When a job closes, get a clean PDF summary of all costs, hours, and performance vs. budget (Pro plan).
